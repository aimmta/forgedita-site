Conformance

A claim should be specific enough to test.

ForgeDITA publishes a bounded DITA 1.3 conformance statement with claimed, partial, unsupported, and evidence-backed behavior.

ForgeDITA does not use “DITA compliant” as an unqualified marketing statement. The public conformance boundary names the DITA track, current claims, partial areas, exclusions, and evidence strategy.

Versioned boundary

Claims belong to a named product and evidence state.

Executable fixtures

Specification, specialization, round-trip, processor, isolation, reproducibility, and negative suites support promotion.

Visible limitations

Partial and unsupported behavior remains public instead of being folded into broad language.

Fail-closed evidence

Incomplete, unavailable, and failed results cannot silently become passing claims.
