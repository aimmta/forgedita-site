# ForgeDITA Blog Content Plan

The repository includes 24 complete articles in `content/blog/`. Six are published on the site and 18 are held as ready-to-review drafts.

## Published

- Publishing Drift Is an Architecture Problem
- A Public DITA Conformance Statement Should Be Normal
- Portability Is a Product Feature, Not an Exit Strategy
- A CCMS Is a Dependency Graph, Not a Folder Tree
- Immutable Toolchain Bundles Make Publishing Reproducible
- Six Questions to Ask During a CCMS Demonstration

## Draft series

The remaining articles cover five themes:

- DITA conformance and content portability
- Graph-aware content management and dependency resolution
- Reproducible publishing and operational reliability
- Extensible architecture and integration boundaries
- Practical CCMS evaluation, adoption, and governance

Each draft contains front matter with its title, slug, description, status, author, and planned sequence. The sequence is editorial guidance, not a fixed publication date, so posts can be reordered around product milestones and customer conversations.

## Recommended cadence

Publish one article every two weeks. Support each article with two or three LinkedIn posts: a problem statement, a technical observation, and a link to the article. When a beta release demonstrates the subject, pair the article with a short YouTube walkthrough.
